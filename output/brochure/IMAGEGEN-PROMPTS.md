# ImageGen prompts

Mode: built-in ImageGen.

## Introduction and concept spread

Use case: ads-marketing  
Asset type: landscape double-page artwork for pages 2-3 of an A5 contemporary art exhibition catalogue  
Primary request: Create a soft, poetic conceptual spread about inherited survival habits passing through generations. It should support an exhibition essay about an elderly woman saving cardboard and refusing air conditioning, and an older generation believing hardship always leads to prosperity. No typography in the image.  
Scene/backdrop: one continuous warm archival-paper field that moves from domestic memory on the left into an underwater biological inheritance diagram on the right. Left side contains ghostlike fragments of flattened cardboard, a palm fan, an empty porcelain bowl, translucent plastic bottles and the edge of a family dining table, all treated as memory traces rather than literal product photography. These traces dissolve into schools of tiny fish and flowing water lines across the center. On the right, the fish currents gently form a large incomplete triangle and three nested generational rings, suggesting speed, body size, stamina, habitus and path dependence without labels.  
Subject: no people and no faces; objects are quiet evidence of past environments. A single amber thread passes through cardboard fibers, water currents, fish bodies, and the triangle, showing history becoming instinct.  
Style/medium: contemporary museum exhibition catalogue, refined art-book collage, analogue photogram, translucent ink wash, graphite annotations without readable writing, subtle cyanotype-like depth but kept in warm neutral tones, tactile paper fibers, delicate and humane rather than technical.  
Composition/framing: wide landscape 3:2 or wider spanning two facing A5 pages; center gutter calm and low-detail; left half has generous negative space for a Chinese essay; right half has a spacious organic triangle and room for explanatory text blocks; elegant asymmetry, layered depth, soft edges.  
Lighting/mood: intimate, empathetic, contemplative; morning window light filtered through water; no melodrama.  
Color palette: warm ivory `#EEE9DE`, near-black `#0F0E0C`, oxidized amber `#C4892A`, restrained muted rust and graphite gray; overall lighter and softer than the cover.  
Materials/textures: old paper, cardboard fibers, translucent water, graphite, faded ink, porcelain, delicate film grain.  
Constraints: image only; absolutely no words, letters, numbers, logos, QR codes, frames, watermark, or mockup; no hard infographic boxes; no rigid corporate geometry; no neon cyberpunk; no cute cartoon fish; no literal portraits; avoid clutter; preserve calm areas for deterministic Chinese typesetting.

## Cover artwork

Use case: ads-marketing  
Asset type: portrait cover artwork for an A5 contemporary art exhibition catalogue  
Primary request: Create an abstract, poetic underwater artwork for an interactive art project titled Downstream, about generational inheritance, path dependence, and survival strategies becoming future constraints. No typography in the image.  
Scene/backdrop: near-black deep water fading into aged warm ivory, like an archaeological cut through layers of time; a subtle downward current.  
Subject: a school of small fish silhouettes moving downstream through three faint nested triangular force fields; one amber-gold fish leaves a delicate wake that becomes a chain, then a scar, then a river contour. The fish should feel like a real emergent collective rather than decorative icons.  
Style/medium: contemporary museum exhibition catalogue, restrained conceptual art, analogue photogram + ink wash + scientific archive plate, subtle film grain, imperfect paper fibers, refined editorial art direction.  
Composition/framing: vertical A5 ratio, strong negative space in the upper third and lower margin for later typography; image concentrated diagonally through the middle; elegant asymmetry.  
Lighting/mood: low-key, meditative, solemn but not horror; underwater particulate light.  
Color palette: `#0F0E0C` near-black, `#DDD8CC` warm ivory, `#C4892A` oxidized amber, tiny muted rust accents only.  
Materials/textures: translucent water, archival paper, graphite, oxidized metal, fine grain.  
Constraints: image only, absolutely no words, letters, numbers, logo, QR code, frame, watermark, or mockup; avoid commercial poster clichés; avoid neon cyberpunk; avoid cute cartoon fish; preserve generous clean areas for deterministic Chinese typesetting later.

## Three generations spread

Use case: ads-marketing  
Asset type: landscape double-page spread artwork for pages 4-5 of an A5 contemporary art exhibition catalogue  
Primary request: Create one continuous conceptual underwater panorama representing three successive historical environments in a single river of time: scarcity, expansion, and stagnation. No typography in the image.  
Scene/backdrop: left third is a fractured dark underwater world with sparse particles and distant predator shadows; center third opens into a warm amber abundance where fish expand and some begin hunting formerly parallel fish; right third contracts into a shallow cold wheel-rut pool, quiet, sparse, and low-energy. The transitions must flow continuously, not look like three separate panels.  
Subject: the same lineage of fish crosses the entire panorama, gradually changing body proportions; faint inherited wake-lines and multiplication-like branching traces connect generations; local panic ripples appear as subtle concentric interruptions. A restrained triangular geometry is embedded under the water like an exhibition diagram.  
Style/medium: contemporary museum biennial catalogue, conceptual environmental art, analogue photogram + ink wash + scientific field archive, subtle film grain and paper fibers, sophisticated editorial art direction.  
Composition/framing: wide landscape 3:2 or wider, designed to span two facing A5 pages with the center gutter kept visually quiet; clear left-to-right narrative; generous calm areas for later captions along top and bottom edges.  
Lighting/mood: left compressed and survivalist, center radiant and seductive, right pale cold and exhausted; poetic rather than literal.  
Color palette: `#0F0E0C` near-black, `#DDD8CC` warm ivory, `#C4892A` oxidized amber, muted rust and desaturated moss accents; no bright blue.  
Materials/textures: archival paper, graphite, oxidized metal, submerged sediment, translucent water.  
Constraints: image only, absolutely no words, letters, numbers, logo, QR code, frame, watermark, or mockup; no literal Chinese myth characters; no cute fish; no neon cyberpunk; no infographic labels; avoid a hard triptych border; preserve a calm center gutter.
